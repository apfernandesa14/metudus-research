# Metudus — Europe Capital Markets Research

Research infrastructure for the Metudus article **“Quanto custa à Europa não ter um verdadeiro mercado de capitais?”**

## Research question

Why does a European economy with substantial savings and external financing continue to struggle to mobilise capital for companies at scale?

## Working thesis

The evidence does not point to a simple shortage of capital. The central issue investigated is the transformation of European savings into equity and growth capital at sufficient scale, depth and integration.

The analysis tests this thesis against competing explanations, including differences in corporate profitability, sector composition, technological concentration and market fragmentation.

## Repository structure

- `sources.md` — human-readable register of sources, claims and page references.
- `data/` — raw or processed datasets used in the research.
- `outputs/` — published exhibits and other research outputs.

## Main sources

The research relies primarily on publications and datasets from:

- European Investment Bank (EIB / BEI)
- European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA)
- European Central Bank (ECB / BCE)
- Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)
- European Commission

The exact document, URL and page reference for each claim are recorded in `sources.md`.
